Display Settings

Orientation, mirroring, panel color order / init sequence and backlight. Each block saves on its own — press its Save button (backlight saves when you release the slider).

Rotation Modes 4 to 7 are mirrored variants supported by the display driver.
Mirror Use these when text appears mirrored or upside down even after choosing a rotation mode.
Panel Color / Init Toggle these to A/B test color tint against the old sd2 firmware: BGR swaps red/blue, the sd2 init skips vendor gamma/power tuning.
Backlight Drag to preview, release to save and apply instantly via PWM (kept across reboots).
